Research foundation

SAGET is the applied training arm of the SAGE ecosystem. The SAGE framework is a validated pedagogical approach to structured AI use, developed through more than eight empirical studies involving over 800 participants across Australian universities, and recognised by TEQSA in the national Generative AI Knowledge Hub. The workshops and consulting engagements offered through SAGET are informed by that foundation, adapted for professional and organisational settings rather than higher education.
