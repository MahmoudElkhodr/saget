Sprints & Playbooks

Workflow consulting and tangible deliverables.

For teams that need more than a workshop. These engagements produce a written deliverable your staff can use after the session ends.

Workflow Sprint

Workflow and Prompt Playbook

A focused engagement where two or three of your team's most time-intensive workflows are mapped, opportunities for AI support are identified, and a custom prompt library is built for your specific business context.

  • Workflow mapping session (up to two hours)
  • Identification of AI-supported task opportunities by role
  • Custom prompt library for your most common work scenarios
  • Written Playbook with guidance on adapting prompts as tools evolve
  • One follow-up review session within four weeks

Timeline: typically two to three weeks from initial scoping call.

From $3,500 +GST
